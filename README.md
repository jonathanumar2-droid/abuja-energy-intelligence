# Kaduna–Abuja Corridor Energy Intelligence Project

### Community Renewable Energy Infrastructure Data and Preliminary Analysis

**Project Category:** Independent Energy Research  
**Research Focus:** Renewable Energy, Energy Infrastructure, Data Analysis  
**Geographical Focus:** Communities along the Kaduna–Abuja corridor, Nigeria  
**Project Status:** Initial Data Collection and Documentation  
**Researcher:** Jonathan Umar  
**Year Initiated:** 2026

---

## 1. About the Project

The Kaduna–Abuja Corridor Energy Intelligence Project is an independent research initiative focused on understanding community-level renewable energy infrastructure, electricity demand and energy system configurations in Nigeria.

The project aims to explore how structured energy data, infrastructure analysis and evidence-based research can contribute to better energy planning, improved electricity access and more effective deployment of renewable energy solutions.

The initial phase uses an existing dataset containing solar photovoltaic (PV), battery storage, maximum power point tracking (MPPT) and inverter capacity information for 17 community energy system records along the Kaduna–Abuja corridor.

Rather than focusing exclusively on the sale or installation of renewable energy products, this initiative seeks to investigate the wider energy ecosystem through data collection, technical analysis and infrastructure intelligence.

## 2. Project Objectives

The main objectives are to:

- Develop a structured database of community renewable energy infrastructure.
- Examine the relationship between solar PV, battery storage, MPPT and inverter capacities.
- Organize and analyse available community energy system data.
- Identify information gaps affecting community energy planning.
- Develop preliminary insights into renewable energy system configurations.
- Expand the database through additional field research and surveys.
- Explore opportunities for improved energy access and infrastructure planning in Nigeria.

## 3. Initial Dataset

The first phase contains 17 community energy system records.

The available variables include:

| Variable | Unit |
|---|---|
| Average Daily Load | Wh (unconfirmed) |
| Total PV Capacity | kWp |
| MPPT Capacity | kW |
| Battery Capacity | kWh |
| Inverter Capacity | kW |

Additional information such as geographical coordinates, household numbers, grid availability, actual electricity generation and operational performance will be collected in subsequent research phases.

## 4. Initial Research Questions

This project will initially investigate:

1. How do renewable energy system capacities vary across the recorded communities?
2. What relationships exist between solar PV capacity and battery storage capacity?
3. How do inverter and MPPT capacities compare with installed PV capacity?
4. What additional data is required to assess the adequacy of existing community energy systems?
5. How can community-level energy data support future infrastructure planning?

## 5. Methodology

The initial research involves:

- Compilation of available community energy system figures.
- Data organization and standardization.
- Development of a structured data dictionary.
- Descriptive statistical analysis.
- Calculation of selected system capacity ratios.
- Identification of missing information and data limitations.

Future phases will incorporate field surveys, geographical information, electricity availability records and additional operational data.

## 6. Project Limitations

The current dataset represents system configuration and design information rather than independently verified operational performance.

Important limitations include:

- The unit and measurement basis of average daily load have not yet been independently confirmed.
- Household and community population figures are unavailable.
- Geographic coordinates and administrative locations are incomplete.
- Actual daily PV generation and electricity consumption figures are unavailable.
- Grid availability and backup generator usage have not yet been documented.
- Some design dates are inferred from community labels and require verification.

These limitations will guide subsequent data collection and research.

## 7. Project Development Roadmap

### Phase 1: Initial Data Organization
- Compile existing community energy records.
- Structure the database.
- Document data definitions and limitations.

### Phase 2: Preliminary Analysis
- Conduct descriptive statistical analysis.
- Compare renewable energy system configurations.
- Develop charts and visualizations.

### Phase 3: Field Research
- Collect additional community energy information.
- Document grid availability and electricity expenditure.
- Obtain household and community-level information.

### Phase 4: Energy Intelligence Development
- Develop community energy profiles.
- Investigate infrastructure gaps.
- Explore energy efficiency and storage opportunities.

### Phase 5: Research Publication
- Produce periodic community energy reports.
- Publish approved datasets.
- Share research findings and recommendations.

## 8. Long-Term Vision

The long-term vision is to develop a growing community energy intelligence resource that can contribute to better understanding of electricity demand, renewable energy infrastructure and energy access challenges in Nigeria.

The project will progressively expand its geographical coverage and analytical capabilities as additional data becomes available.
