# Research Methodology

## 1. Research Approach

The Kaduna–Abuja Corridor Energy Intelligence Project adopts a descriptive and exploratory research approach.

The initial research focuses on organizing existing community renewable energy system information and examining relationships between the capacities of major system components.

The project will progressively incorporate primary field data to support more comprehensive analysis of community electricity demand and energy infrastructure.

## 2. Initial Data Collection

The first dataset contains 17 community energy system records.

The available information was compiled from system design and load figures supplied to the researcher through work involving SOSAI Renewable Energy.

The variables currently available are:

- Average Daily Load (Wh): Measurement basis unconfirmed.
- Total PV Capacity (kWp).
- MPPT Capacity (kW).
- Battery Capacity (kWh).
- Inverter Capacity (kW).

No missing geographical or operational values have been assumed or fabricated.

## 3. Data Organization

The collected information is organized into a structured spreadsheet.

Each record represents one community energy system entry.

The database separates:

- Original supplied information.
- Standardized community labels.
- Inferred design dates.
- Calculated technical indicators.
- Information requiring further collection.

## 4. Data Processing

The initial processing stage involves:

1. Standardizing column names.
2. Checking numerical values.
3. Identifying missing information.
4. Documenting measurement units.
5. Identifying inferred information.
6. Calculating selected system capacity ratios.

Original source values are retained separately from calculated indicators.

## 5. Analytical Indicators

The initial analysis uses three ratios.

### Battery-to-PV Capacity Ratio

Battery Capacity (kWh) / Total PV Capacity (kWp)

This indicator describes the nominal battery storage capacity relative to PV array capacity.

Unit: kWh/kWp.

### Inverter-to-PV Capacity Ratio

Inverter Capacity (kW) / Total PV Capacity (kWp)

This indicator compares inverter power capacity with solar PV peak capacity.

Unit: kW/kWp.

### MPPT-to-PV Capacity Ratio

MPPT Capacity (kW) / Total PV Capacity (kWp)

This indicator compares MPPT capacity with total PV array capacity.

Unit: kW/kWp.

These ratios describe system configuration and should not be interpreted as direct measures of efficiency or actual performance.

## 6. Descriptive Statistical Analysis

The initial analysis examines:

- Total recorded PV capacity.
- Average PV capacity.
- Median PV capacity.
- Minimum and maximum PV capacity.
- Total battery capacity.
- Average battery capacity.
- Total inverter capacity.
- Average inverter capacity.
- Distribution of MPPT capacity.

Further statistical analysis will be conducted as the dataset expands.

## 7. Data Limitations

The current dataset does not provide sufficient information to establish:

- Actual daily electricity generation.
- Actual daily electricity consumption.
- System availability or uptime.
- Battery state of health.
- Battery depth of discharge.
- Actual system efficiency.
- Community-wide electricity access rates.
- The proportion of households served.

The average daily load measurement basis must also be confirmed before comparing it with system capacities.

## 8. Future Research Methodology

Future research stages will incorporate:

- Community-level field surveys.
- Household energy consumption information.
- Grid availability observations.
- Generator usage and fuel expenditure.
- Geographical mapping.
- Solar resource information.
- Actual system performance records.

The expanded dataset will support more detailed assessments of community energy infrastructure and potential energy planning interventions.

## 9. Research Integrity

All analysis will distinguish between original observations, calculated values and estimates.

Third-party information will be handled in accordance with applicable permissions and confidentiality requirements.

Findings will be presented with their assumptions, limitations and data sources clearly documented.
